## About me

I'm Alex, a 32-year-old backend developer based in **Barcelona**. I've spent years building APIs and systems with **.NET**, and lately I've been diving deep into two worlds that have completely taken over my spare time: **cybersecurity** and **AI**.

I approach tech with a security-first mindset — got my Google Cybersecurity cert and SC-900 under the belt, currently working toward Security+. On the AI side, I'm all about self-hosted models, local LLMs with Ollama, and building agents that actually do something useful.

I'm a big believer in **FOSS** and self-hosting. If something can run on my own hardware instead of someone else's cloud, that's where it lives.

When I'm not at the keyboard: **NewJeans** on the speakers, learning **Rust** on the side, and probably breaking something I just fixed.
